TenantIQ Teams Hardening v4

Purpose:
Resolve Microsoft Graph / MSAL assembly collisions such as:

Method 'GetTokenAsync' in type
'Microsoft.Graph.PowerShell.Authentication.Core.Utilities.UserProvidedTokenCredential'
... does not have an implementation.

Architecture change:
- Teams/Exchange/Purview remain in the main TenantIQ PowerShell process.
- Graph-dependent Teams controls no longer call Connect-MgGraph in that process.
- A clean child PowerShell process collects all required Graph evidence once.
- PowerShell 7 (pwsh.exe) is preferred when installed.
- Windows PowerShell (powershell.exe) is the fallback.
- The child process writes a JSON evidence cache to:
  00 Runtime\Teams-Graph-Evidence.json
- Graph-dependent Teams checks consume that cache.
- The cache is cleared at the start of every full Teams assessment.

Graph evidence collected:
- Teams-backed Microsoft 365 groups
- Group lifecycle policies
- Intune managed device compliance
- Default Entra cross-tenant access policy

This design isolates Microsoft.Graph MSAL assemblies from ExchangeOnlineManagement,
MicrosoftTeams, SharePoint Online Management Shell, and Purview assemblies.
