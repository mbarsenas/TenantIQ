# TenantIQ - Understanding Results

TenantIQ uses PASS, WARNING, FAIL, INFO, and NOT EVALUATED statuses.

PASS = evaluated control met the TenantIQ criteria.
WARNING = evaluated control requires review or improvement.
FAIL = evaluated control did not meet the TenantIQ criteria.
INFO = contextual, unscored evidence.
NOT EVALUATED = required evidence was unavailable or unsupported.

Scoring uses PASS = full credit, WARNING = half credit, FAIL = no credit. INFO and NOT EVALUATED are excluded from scored-control math.

Portfolio labels:

  90-100  Strong Posture
  75-89   Needs Attention
  60-74   Elevated Risk
  0-59    High Risk

A standardized FAIL normally represents a tenant finding, not a TenantIQ runtime failure. Runtime failures appear as authentication errors, missing modules/cmdlets/functions, exceptions, or terminated workload processes.

Review FAIL findings first, followed by high-severity WARNING findings, remaining WARNING findings, NOT EVALUATED coverage gaps, and INFO context.
