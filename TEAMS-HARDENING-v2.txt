   - Only explicit legacy Skype for Business coexistence modes are flagged.
3. Added dedicated evaluation paths for:
   - Teams Messaging Policies
   - User Chat Controls
   - Giphy and Meme Controls
   - Teams Channel Policies
   - Private Channel Creation
   - Shared Channel Creation
   - PSTN Usage Configuration
   - Emergency Call Routing
4. Preserved INFO for controls where a universal PASS/FAIL would require licensing,
   Purview/Entra/Intune data, activity telemetry, or an organization-approved baseline.
5. INFO results remain excluded from TenantIQ score calculation.
