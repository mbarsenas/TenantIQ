TenantIQ Teams Hardening v4.6

Fix:
The tenant's installed Get-CsOnlineUser implementation rejected both:
- -ResultSize Unlimited
- -ResultSize ([uint32]::MaxValue)

The latter overflowed a System.Int32 parameter.

v4.6 now uses:
Get-CsOnlineUser -ResultSize 1000000

This is a safe Int32-compatible upper bound and is more than sufficient for
TenantIQ user enumeration in practical Microsoft 365 tenants.

No other Teams assessment logic was changed.
