TenantIQ Teams Hardening v4.4

Fix:
Tenant validation confirmed that Get-CsOnlineUser directly exposes:
- TeamsUpgradeEffectiveMode
- TeamsUpgradePolicy
- TeamsUpgradeOverridePolicy
- TeamsUpgradeNotificationsEnabled
- TeamsUpgradePolicyIsReadOnly

The v4.3 Teams Upgrade Policy control attempted to use the -Properties parameter,
which is not required and can behave inconsistently across MicrosoftTeams module
versions.

v4.4 now retrieves users with:

Get-CsOnlineUser -ResultSize Unlimited

and reads TeamsUpgradeEffectiveMode / TeamsUpgradePolicy directly from each user.

Expected result:
- PASS when no users have an effective legacy mode.
- WARNING when one or more users have:
    SfBOnly
    SfBWithTeamsCollab
    SfBWithTeamsCollabAndMeetings
- Global Islands remains informational context and is not automatically penalized.
