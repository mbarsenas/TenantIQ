# TenantIQ - SharePoint Online

SharePoint Online includes 50 registered TenantIQ controls covering tenant configuration, site governance, sharing, access, storage, lifecycle, and operational posture.

TenantIQ uses the SharePoint Online Management Shell and may request the tenant name before assessment.

Typical tenant admin endpoint:

  https://<tenant>-admin.sharepoint.com

Run SharePoint Online from main menu option [3]. Complete Microsoft authentication when prompted and allow all 50 checks to finish.

Some site-level evidence can require additional administrative access beyond a successful tenant-level connection. When evidence cannot be retrieved, TenantIQ reports the limitation rather than assuming the site is healthy or unhealthy.

The workload exports a timestamped CSV under 06 Output.

OneDrive assessment also relies on SharePoint administrative connectivity because OneDrive for Business is built on SharePoint Online infrastructure.
