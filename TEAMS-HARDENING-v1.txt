TenantIQ Teams Hardening v1

This package upgrades the Teams assessment from generic inventory-only INFO results
to check-specific scoring for controls where Microsoft Teams PowerShell exposes a
clear, authoritative configuration signal.

Hardened/scored areas include:
- Tenant connectivity/configuration
- Upgrade policy
- Meeting policy inventory
- Anonymous meeting join
- External meeting join
- Meeting recording/transcription posture
- Lobby configuration
- Federation posture
- Guest calling
- App permission/setup policy presence
- Team ownership coverage
- Ownerless teams
- Single-owner teams
- Guest membership inventory
- Calling policy inventory
- Emergency calling policy presence
- Voice routing/dial plan/caller ID inventory

Inventory/context-dependent controls remain INFO by design. This prevents TenantIQ
from inventing PASS/FAIL results for controls that require Purview, Entra, Intune,
activity telemetry, licensing, or an organization-specific approved baseline.

The next hardening pass should use the first real tenant run to refine property names,
licensing-dependent behavior, and organization-specific thresholds.
