TenantIQ Teams Hardening v4.3

Fixes the Teams Upgrade Policy false-positive discovered during tenant validation.

Previous behavior:
Get-CsTeamsUpgradePolicy returned all Microsoft built-in policy definitions, and
TenantIQ counted built-in SfBOnly/SfBWithTeamsCollab definitions as active legacy
configuration.

Correct behavior in v4.3:
- Reads the Global TeamsUpgradePolicy separately.
- Enumerates users with Get-CsOnlineUser.
- Evaluates TeamsUpgradeEffectiveMode / TeamsUpgradePolicy at the user level.
- Built-in policy definitions are never scored merely because they exist.
- WARNING only when one or more users actually have an effective legacy mode:
    SfBOnly
    SfBWithTeamsCollab
    SfBWithTeamsCollabAndMeetings
- PASS when no users have an effective legacy coexistence mode.
- Global Islands is reported as context, not automatically scored as a warning.

Microsoft documentation explicitly states that the relevant TeamsUpgradePolicy
instances in Microsoft 365 are built-in system policies and that the Global policy
applies unless a user is explicitly assigned another policy.
