TenantIQ Teams Hardening v4.1

Fix:
The isolated Graph collector was previously stored under 01 Framework.
TenantIQ loads framework .ps1 files during startup, which caused PowerShell
to execute the collector directly and prompt for its mandatory OutputPath parameter.

The collector has been moved to:
00 Runtime\Tools\Invoke-TenantIQGraphIsolatedCache.ps1

The Teams hardened evaluator now launches that script only when a Graph-dependent
Teams control needs isolated Microsoft Graph evidence.

Expected startup behavior:
- TenantIQ launches normally.
- No OutputPath prompt appears.
- When the first Graph-dependent Teams check runs, TenantIQ launches the isolated
  child PowerShell process and supplies OutputPath automatically.
