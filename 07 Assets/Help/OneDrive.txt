# TenantIQ - OneDrive

OneDrive includes 50 registered TenantIQ controls covering OneDrive for Business governance, sharing, storage, lifecycle, access, and operational posture.

Run OneDrive from main menu option [5].

Because OneDrive for Business is built on SharePoint Online, TenantIQ uses SharePoint administrative connectivity and Microsoft Graph evidence where required.

TenantIQ may request the SharePoint tenant name before assessment.

Evidence availability can depend on SharePoint administrative access, Graph permissions, licensing, and whether OneDrive resources have been provisioned.

The workload exports a timestamped CSV under 06 Output.

A lack of provisioned OneDrive objects can legitimately result in informational findings rather than authentication failure.
