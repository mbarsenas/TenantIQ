# TenantIQ - Microsoft Teams

Microsoft Teams includes 50 registered TenantIQ controls covering tenant settings, meeting and messaging policies, governance, security, external access, and operational posture.

Required module:

  MicrosoftTeams

Run Teams from main menu option [4]. Complete Microsoft authentication when prompted and allow all 50 checks to finish.

TenantIQ may use both MicrosoftTeams PowerShell and Microsoft Graph evidence depending on the control.

Licensing, permissions, administrator consent, and Microsoft service/API availability can affect individual results.

The workload exports a timestamped CSV under 06 Output.

INFO and NOT EVALUATED results are unscored context. Review them when determining assessment coverage completeness.
